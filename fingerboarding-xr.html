Fingerboarding XR combines direct hand interaction, responsive board physics and a procedural skatepark that can be reshaped in real time.

Direct interaction

The player controls the virtual skateboard directly with their hands. Finger-scale contact replaces conventional buttons and controllers, keeping the interaction close to the gestures of physical fingerboarding.

Picking up the board, positioning the fingers and attempting a trick all happen in the same mixed reality space, without switching interaction modes.

Board physics

The physics system turns small hand movements into force, rotation and momentum. The board responds continuously to finger input, the table and the geometry of each obstacle rather than relying on predefined trick animations.

This makes every attempt slightly different and keeps balance, timing, collisions and recovery at the centre of the experience.

Real-time procedural generation

The environment is built from procedural obstacles instead of fixed assets. In-world controls let the player place a ramp on the physical surface and modify its form and proportions while the geometry updates in real time.

The generated obstacle remains immediately playable, creating a continuous loop between level design and interaction: reshape the ramp, test a line and adjust it again without leaving mixed reality.
