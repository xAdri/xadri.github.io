· Mixcare ·

Mixed Reality & Biosensors for Preoperative Anxiety

Mixcare is a mixed reality experience that helps patients manage anxiety before surgery while remaining connected to their real surroundings. Non-invasive sensors capture heart rate and breathing in real time, represented through an emotional avatar that supports guided relaxation. A clinical pilot at La Fe University and Polytechnic Hospital evaluates its comfort, effectiveness, and potential integration into preoperative care.

CE-Marked Class IIa Mobile Therapeutic App

Sea of Heroes: New World is a CE-marked Class IIa digital therapeutic for children with combined-type ADHD. Through personalized serious games, its adaptive AI adjusts difficulty to each child’s progress during short sessions prescribed and monitored by neuropsychology professionals.
